Pick two colors and a pattern. Preview it tiled at any zoom, export a clean PNG tile for your game or pixel art.

How to use

The exported PNG is exactly one pattern tile (e.g. 4×4 px for Bayer 4×4) at 1 pixel per cell — drop it straight into a tilemap or use it as a background-image with background-repeat: repeat. Zoom only affects the on-screen preview.
